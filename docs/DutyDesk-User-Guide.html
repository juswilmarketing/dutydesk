1. Introduction

DutyDesk is a web application for Trinidad & Tobago customs brokerage clerks. It helps you:

  • Upload commercial invoices (PDF or scanned images)
  • Extract line items automatically using AI
  • Match products to official T&T tariff codes (6,000+ entries)
  • Calculate import duty, VAT, CIF, and container fees
  • Generate and send tax advice to consignees by email or WhatsApp
  • Share consignee records and sent worksheets across the team

DutyDesk runs in your browser. Your team shares one database for consignees, exchange rates, and classification history. Invoice data in your current session is cleared when you sign out.

2. Signing in

  1. Open your DutyDesk URL in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
  2. Enter your username and password provided by your administrator.
  3. Click Sign in.
Tip: On first login, change the default admin password if your site still uses the factory credentials. Contact your administrator for account setup.

Sign out (top right) clears invoice data stored in your browser for privacy. Team consignees and sent reports remain in the shared database.

4. Workflow at a glance

UPLOAD invoice(s) → REVIEW line items & tariff codes → TAXES: enter freight / charges / exchange rate → SEND tax advice (email or WhatsApp) → optional: ASYCUDA entry · Brokerage fee quote

Most shipments follow this path. Manual entry is used when you have no invoice document or need to add a few items quickly.

5. Upload — supplier invoices

5.1 Supported files

  • PDF — single or multi-page; multiple invoices in one PDF are split automatically
  • Images — JPG, PNG, WEBP (scanned invoices)
  • Maximum file size: approximately 10 MB

5.2 Steps

  1. Go to the Upload tab.
  2. Drag and drop a file onto the drop zone, or click to browse.
  3. Wait while DutyDesk: reads the file → extracts line items → matches the tariff database → classifies remaining items with AI.
  4. You are taken automatically to Review when processing completes.

5.3 Multiple invoices

If a PDF contains more than one invoice (different invoice numbers or layouts), each appears as a separate entry in Review. The filename shows a tag such as invoice.pdf · INV-12345.

Tip: For best scan results, use clear, straight photos with all columns visible — especially the line extension/total column on the right.

6. Review & classify line items

6.1 Invoice sidebar

Select an invoice from the left panel. Use + Add Invoice to upload more files.

6.2 Invoice details

Edit supplier name, invoice number, date, ship-from/to, currency, and exchange rate if needed.

  • Currency — set if the invoice is not in USD (EUR, GBP, CAD, CNY, TTD).
  • Rate (1 CUR = TT$) — required for non-USD invoices before tax calculation.

6.3 Line item table

Hover column headers for explanations. Key columns:

ColumnAction
DescriptionClick to edit if AI misread the product name.
T&T Tariff CodeEdit directly, pick from similar codes, or re-classify with AI (↻ button).
SourceOfficial = database match · AI est. = verify at ttbizlink.gov.tt
Qty / Unit Price / TotalShould match the supplier invoice. Total uses the printed line extension when available.

6.4 Export from Review

  • Export CSV — spreadsheet of line items with tariff codes
  • Export Report — printable tariff classification report with your clerk name

7. Manual entry

Use when you need to classify items without a full invoice upload.

  1. Go to Manual.
  2. Type a product description — the tariff database is searched as you type.
  3. Select a match from the dropdown, or click + Add Item to use AI classification.
  4. Enter quantity, unit, and unit price (USD).

Items attach to the currently selected invoice, or a new Quick Entry list is created.

8. Tax calculator

8.1 Select invoice(s)

  • Choose one invoice from the dropdown, or check Combine all invoices to calculate multiple shipments together.
  • For non-USD invoices, confirm currency and TT$ exchange rate are set.

8.2 Customs tax inputs

FieldDescription
Invoice Total (Auto)Sum of classified line items (converted to USD if needed).
FreightInternational shipping — added to CIF.
InsuranceCargo insurance — added to CIF.
Exchange RateCustoms rate: 1 USD = TT$. Up to 5 decimal places. Blank uses team default.
Container Size20ft (TT$750) or 40ft (TT$1,050) CES exam fee, if applicable.
User FeeTT$80 UFC fee when checked.
VAT ExemptCheck if entire shipment is VAT exempt.

8.3 Tax breakdown

The itemized table shows each line's share of CIF (TTD), duty, and VAT. Use checkboxes to mark individual lines as duty-exempt or VAT-exempt. Override duty/VAT manually in Tax Advice Details if needed.

8.4 Consignee

Click Manage to add or edit consignees (name, email, phone, address, BIR code). Consignee records sync across the team. The consignee name must appear on certified cheques.

9. Freight, insurance & other charges

On the Taxes tab, use the two sub-tabs under Customs Tax Inputs:

Freight & Insurance

Enter amounts detected on the invoice or type manually. Values feed into CIF calculation.

Other Charges

Sales tax, VAT/GST on the invoice, handling fees, and similar charges appear here when detected during upload. You can add or edit each charge and mark Include in CIF when the amount should be part of the customs value.

Charges detected at upload (freight, insurance, sales tax) are stored on the invoice and carry through to tax calculation automatically.

10. Invoice totals check

After upload, DutyDesk compares the sum of line items to the goods subtotal or grand total printed on the supplier invoice.

If totals do not match: A yellow warning appears on Review. Click Review now to see the difference. Fix missing lines, quantities, or prices before sending tax advice.

If you acknowledge the discrepancy after checking, click I've reviewed — continue. Tax advice may note that line items and invoice totals may not be in sync.

11. Sending tax advice

11.1 Prepare

  1. Complete tax calculation on the Taxes tab.
  2. Fill in Worksheet #, Bill of Lading, and commodity description.
  3. Select the consignee (with email and/or phone).
  4. Click Send Tax Advice.

11.2 Send modal options

  • Email — sends from configured Gmail when direct send is enabled; otherwise opens your mail client with attachments.
  • WhatsApp — when consignee phone is saved, opens WhatsApp with a summary; email may send automatically first.
  • EC75 notice — adds signing instructions for the EC75 form.
  • Tax calculation breakdown — optional second attachment explaining CIF, duty, and VAT step by step.
  • Attachments — add B/L, signed forms, or other PDFs manually.

11.3 Other exports

  • Print — tax advice document for in-office use
  • Itemized Report — detailed duty/VAT breakdown per line
  • Send to ASYCUDA — passes approved figures to the ASYCUDA tab
Payment rules on the tax advice: under TT$5,000 — cash or certified cheque; over TT$5,000 — certified cheque only, payable to THE COMPTROLLER OF CUSTOMS & EXCISE.

12. Brokerage fees

The Brokerage tab estimates your brokerage service charges from CIF value, discount settings, and fee schedule. Use alongside tax calculation for a complete client quote.

13. ASYCUDA handoff

After approving tax figures on the Taxes tab, click Send to ASYCUDA →. The ASYCUDA tab receives line items with tariff codes, CIF (TTD/USD), duty, VAT, and consignee for customs entry preparation.

14. Learned classifications

When you confirm or correct a tariff code in Review, DutyDesk saves the description-to-code mapping for the whole team. Future uploads matching that description use the learned code automatically (Learned badge).

View and manage all learned entries on the Learned tab.

15. Reports & team sync

Every tax advice sent is logged on Reports with worksheet number, consignee, amounts, method (email/WhatsApp), and attachments. Logs sync to the shared database on login and when you send new advice.

Consignees and exchange rate also sync across clerks.

16. Admin — user accounts

Administrators can access the Users tab to:

  • Create clerk and admin accounts
  • Set display names (shown on tax advice and reports)
  • Reset passwords

17. Tips & troubleshooting

IssueWhat to do
Review tab is greyed outUpload at least one invoice first.
AI classification failsCheck internet connection; contact admin if API key is not configured.
Line totals don't match invoiceEdit qty/prices in Review; check for missing lines or charges counted as products.
Non-USD invoice — wrong taxSet invoice currency and TT$ rate on Review or Taxes tab.
Exchange rate outdatedEnter current Customs Division rate on Taxes tab; optionally save as team default.
Email won't send directlyAdmin must configure Google Apps Script webhook; use Mail App fallback.
Tariff code uncertainVerify at ttbizlink.gov.tt HS Code Tariff Finder.
Previous user's data visibleAlways sign out when leaving the workstation.
