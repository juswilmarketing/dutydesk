Subheading Explanatory Notes.

Subheading 0103.10

For the purposes of subheading 0103.10, the expression "pure-bred breeding animals" covers only those breeding animals which are regarded as "pure-bred" by the competent national authorities.

Subheadings 0103.91 and 0103.92

For the purposes of subheadings 0103.91 and 0103.92, the specified weight limits relate to the weight of each animal.
